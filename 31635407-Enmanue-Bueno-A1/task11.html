Documentation

1a: Lambda is a uniform that adjusts the ratio between points. The implemented code creates a vertex with the color and coordinal position based lambda, the ratio between the two points. A triangle is then created based on the vertex and the in between vertices created. If a different value than 0.5 of lambda is used, the ratio between the will differ from being the middle point of each vertex. When lambda is less than 0.5 the vertices created are based on a smaller ratio of point a and a larger ratio of points b and c repectively, and a smaller ratio of point b and larger ratio of point c. When lambda is greater than 0.6 the vertices created are based on a larger ratio of point a and smaller ratio of point b and c respectively, and a larger ratio of point b and smaller ratio of point c. (Point a is the bottom left point, point b is the top point, and point c is the bottom right point) 1b: The alpha silder changes the color's opacity. This is accomplished by sending a four-dimensional vector with stores red, green, blue and alpha. The GPU pipeline then handles this vector with the according colors and alpha being treated as a tranparency value.
